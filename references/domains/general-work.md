# General Work Domain Pack

Use when the user's chats mix multiple professional functions and no specialized pack fits cleanly.

Core question:

> What work functions does AI support, what value does the human still contribute, and what needs verification?

Look for:

- writing and communication;
- research and analysis;
- planning;
- operations;
- decision support;
- coordination;
- automation;
- learning.

Typical exclusions:

- health/medical;
- pure leisure;
- personal admin if the user wants only professional work;
- sensitive third-party material.

If one specific domain dominates after initial sampling, switch to a domain-specific or custom pack before full labeling.
