# Engineering Domain Pack

Use for engineering students, engineers, technical research, coding-adjacent engineering, hardware, simulation, data/ML engineering, design, debugging, documentation, and verification.

Core question:

> When AI can cheaply generate technical answers or artifacts, what human engineering inputs remain valuable?

Look for:

- problem framing;
- context selection;
- domain knowledge;
- decomposition;
- design tradeoffs;
- verification/testing;
- integration;
- risk ownership;
- learning capital.

Typical exclusions:

- medical/lifestyle questions;
- personal admin;
- creative/leisure;
- finance/legal;
- general education unrelated to engineering.
