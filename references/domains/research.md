# Research Domain Pack

Use for academic or industrial research: literature review, experimental design, methods, data analysis, coding notebooks, academic writing, peer review, and research operations.

Core question:

> Which parts of research work does AI accelerate, and where do human judgment, source evaluation, method design, interpretation, and research integrity remain necessary?

Look for:

- research question framing;
- source evaluation;
- methods design;
- data interpretation;
- reproducibility;
- ethics/privacy judgment;
- manuscript writing;
- peer review.

Typical exclusions:

- personal admin;
- health advice not tied to research;
- finance/legal unrelated to research;
- non-research education.

For publishable claims, require source checking and reproducibility checks.
