# Education Domain Pack

Use for teaching, tutoring, studying, assessment, feedback, lesson planning, curriculum design, education research, and student skill formation.

Core question:

> How does AI change learning, feedback, assessment, and the signaling of real understanding?

Look for:

- tutoring;
- worked examples;
- misconception diagnosis;
- feedback;
- assessment design;
- curriculum alignment;
- student context;
- age appropriateness;
- academic integrity.

Typical exclusions:

- personal admin;
- health/medical;
- finance/legal;
- creative/leisure;
- non-education professional work.

For student data, treat privacy and age appropriateness as high-priority risks.
