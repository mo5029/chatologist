# Finance Domain Pack

Use for accounting, finance, valuation, forecasting, investment analysis, reporting, reconciliation, due diligence, markets, compliance, and financial operations.

Core question:

> Where does AI reduce financial analysis, reporting, or modeling effort, and where do human assumptions, source checks, compliance judgment, and risk ownership remain necessary?

Look for:

- assumption setting;
- source verification;
- numerical checking;
- spreadsheet audit;
- regulatory or compliance judgment;
- risk ownership;
- stakeholder judgment;
- business context.

Typical exclusions:

- personal lifestyle admin;
- health/medical;
- legal issues not tied to finance;
- creative/leisure;
- education unrelated to finance.

Use conservative verification labels. Finance outputs can look precise while being wrong.
